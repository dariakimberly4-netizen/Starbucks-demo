The Starbucks story

A coffee company built around connection.

The original store focused on fresh-roasted whole bean coffee. Over time, the idea grew into something larger: coffeehouses designed as places to gather, talk and belong.

1971

Pike Place beginnings

Starbucks started as a single Seattle store offering high-quality whole bean coffee and building a name around coffee craft.

1981

A first cup changes the direction

Howard Schultz first encountered Starbucks in 1981 and joined the company the following year.

1983

The coffeehouse idea

A trip to Italy inspired a vision of coffeehouses as social spaces—places for conversation, community and a pause between home and work.

1987

The next chapter begins

Schultz returned and acquired Starbucks with local investors, helping turn that coffeehouse vision into a larger company story.

Starbucks in the Philippines

December 4, 1997.

The first Starbucks store in the Philippines opened at 6750 Ayala Building in Makati City. That opening made the Philippines the third Starbucks market outside North America and began a long local chapter shaped by Filipino hospitality, connection and a strong love of coffee.

6750 Ayala Avenue, Makati City — the address that marked the beginning of the Starbucks Experience in the Philippines.

More than coffee

Connection is the thread.

Across coffee, partners, customers, stores and neighborhoods, the idea is consistent: make each interaction feel human, welcoming and purposeful.

01 / COFFEE

Craft with care

Quality coffee begins with sourcing, roasting and a commitment to the people who grow it.

02 / PARTNERS

Belong together

Starbucks refers to employees as partners and emphasizes respect, inclusion and shared responsibility.

03 / CUSTOMERS

Make it personal

Every visit is an opportunity to create a genuine moment of connection around coffee.

04 / STORES

Create a third place

Stores are designed as a pause between home and work—sometimes lively, sometimes quiet, always welcoming.

05 / COMMUNITY

Be a good neighbor

Each store sits inside a larger neighborhood, with a responsibility to contribute positively to the people around it.

06 / POSSIBILITY

Keep moving forward

The story continues through the people, conversations and communities that give the coffeehouse meaning.

The next cup

The story is still being written.

From Pike Place to Makati and beyond, the strongest part of the Starbucks story is not a single building or date. It is the repeated act of people meeting, talking and sharing a cup together.
