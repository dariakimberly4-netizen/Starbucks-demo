1981

Howard Schultz walks into Starbucks, is drawn in by a cup of Sumatra, and joins the company the following year.

Chapter 03 · Milan

1983

A trip to Italy inspires the idea of bringing the warmth, ritual and human connection of Italian coffee bars into the Starbucks experience.

CoffeehouseNot only coffee—conversation, ritual and a place between home and work.

1987

Howard Schultz returns and purchases Starbucks with the help of local investors, beginning a new era of expansion.

1997
The story reaches the Philippines

Makati becomes the next chapter.

The first Starbucks store in the Philippines opened on December 4, 1997 at the 6750 Ayala Building in Makati City, making the Philippines Starbucks’ third market outside North America.

6750 Ayala Building, Makati City — the location of the first Starbucks store in the Philippines.
